# Top Lubricant Products Popular in Kutch, Gujarat

Kutch is one of Gujarat's largest industrial and transport hubs, with extensive logistics, mining, construction, agriculture, and commercial transportation activities. Heavy-duty diesel engine oils are among the most widely used lubricants in the region. The following products from TotalEnergies, Valvoline, and ELF are among the most recognized and commonly used lubricant products in transport fleets, commercial vehicles, and industrial applications.
